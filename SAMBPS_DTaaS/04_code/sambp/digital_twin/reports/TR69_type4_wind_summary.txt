DESIGN NOTES
------------
• GFM/VSM swing equation: M×dω/dt = P_mech – P_elec – D×Δω with M=2H.
  This gives the Siemens SG14 (H=6 s, D=25) a ROCOF of ≈0.017 pu/s for
  a 0.2 pu disturbance — comparable to a conventional SG with H=6 s.

• FRT priority: during deep LVRT (V<0.2 pu) reactive current has priority
  over active power (IEEE 1547-2018 §6.5.2 / IEC 61400-27-1 Annex E).

• Cp polynomial: Heier 6-parameter polynomial (c1..c6), numerically validated
  against GE-Wind Cp tables; Cp_max ≈ 0.48 at λ_opt ≈ 8.1, β=0°.

• DC link: simple capacitor balance I_gen_dc – I_grid_dc with C_dc in mF.
  Vestas 50 mF, Siemens 80 mF, Goldwind 100 mF — sized for 10% ΔV at rated P.
