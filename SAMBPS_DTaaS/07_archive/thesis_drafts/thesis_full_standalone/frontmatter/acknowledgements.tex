I owe a profound debt of gratitude to my doctoral supervisor,
\textbf{Prof.\ K. Shanti Swarup}, Department of Electrical Engineering,
IIT Madras, whose incisive questioning, patient mentorship, and insistence
on rigour pushed this work far beyond what I thought it capable of.
His guidance during the three key pivot points of this research---the

I am grateful to the members of my Doctoral Committee for their

This work benefited greatly from interactions with colleagues in the
Smart Grid and Protection Laboratory, IIT Madras. The simulation
infrastructure, the Monte Carlo validation framework, and the
hardware-in-the-loop test protocol were collectively refined through
countless hours of discussion and collaborative debugging.

Financial support from the Ministry of Education, Government of India,
through the Prime Minister's Research Fellowship (PMRF) is gratefully
acknowledged. Computational resources were provided by the IITM Param
Brahmand HPC facility.

Finally, none of this would have been possible without the unconditional
support of my family. My wife Divya, my parents, and my sister provided
the anchor through every setback and the celebration through every milestone.
