\section*{Plain-Language Statement (for portal upload)}

\textbf{Anoop V. Eluvathingal:} Identified and formalised the slope
adaptation problem as a constrained online parameter estimation task;
derived the physics-feasible set $\mathcal{K}(\kappa_n)$ from the
LM condition number; designed the projected-gradient adaptive law
(Algorithm~1); carried out the complete Lyapunov stability analysis
including Theorem~1, Lemma~1, and Corollary~1; implemented all
simulations on the 33\,kV mixed-IBR test feeder (2400 scenarios);
produced all figures and tables; wrote the manuscript draft.

\textbf{R. K. Prasad:} Supervised the research programme; provided
guidance on the connection between the Lyapunov framework and the
SAMBPS architecture; reviewed and edited the final manuscript;
secured project funding.
