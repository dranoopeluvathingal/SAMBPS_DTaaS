\bigskip\noindent\textbf{Detailed statement.}

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Arun V.\ Eluvathingal (AVE):}
Identified the multi-substation scaling gap in the SAMBPS framework.
Designed the three-layer hierarchical architecture and spectral
clustering partition.  Derived the delayed $\kappa_n$-weighted
consensus weights and the weighted fault-zone voting rule.
Proved Proposition~1 (delayed consensus correctness) using graph-theoretic
weight dominance.  Implemented the Monte Carlo simulation framework on
ANDES (IEEE~39, IEEE~118, synthetic 50-substation), computed all
performance metrics.  Conducted the scalability and cluster-size
sensitivity analyses.  Wrote all sections of the manuscript.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{R.\ K.\ Prasad (RKP):}
Provided research direction and identified the wide-area scaling gap
as a high-priority open problem.  Reviewed the consensus algorithm design
and the convergence proposition.  Provided access to ANDES simulation
infrastructure and RTDS interface.  Edited and approved the
final manuscript.
