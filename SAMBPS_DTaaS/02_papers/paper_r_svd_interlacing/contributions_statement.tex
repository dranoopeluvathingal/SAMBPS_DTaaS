\section*{Plain-Language Statement (for portal upload)}

\textbf{Anoop V. Eluvathingal:} Identified the need for SVD interlacing
bounds for non-orthonormal physics-structured projections arising from
DAE constraint elimination; formulated and proved Theorem~1 (interlacing
with coupling correction), Theorem~2 (condition-number inheritance),
Corollary~1 (sufficient inheritance condition), and Proposition~1
(Physics-Reduction Proposition for the IBR fault-current Jacobian);
proved sharpness of all bounds; carried out all numerical experiments
(1000-point Monte Carlo on the Park $dq$ measurement map); produced
all figures; wrote the manuscript.

\textbf{R. K. Prasad:} Supervised the research; provided physical
intuition connecting the algebraic coupling ratio $\rho$ to generator
inductance parameters; reviewed and edited the final manuscript; secured
project funding.
