\section*{Plain-Language Statement (for portal upload)}

\textbf{Anoop V. Eluvathingal:} Identified the gap in the CUEP
conservativeness proof for IBR-penetrated systems; formulated the
IBR-modified energy function; proved Theorem~1 (conservativeness),
Lemmas~1--2 (CUEP angle and energy monotonicity), Theorem~2 (monotone
conservatism gap), and Proposition~1 (SAMBPS C13 zero-false-trip result);
designed and executed the IEEE 39-bus simulation study (1500 scenarios);
produced all figures and tables; wrote the manuscript draft.

\textbf{R. K. Prasad:} Supervised the research; provided guidance on
the CUEP method literature and the appropriate assumptions for IBR
current-limiter modelling; reviewed and edited the final manuscript;
secured funding through the SAMBP project.
