\subsection{TR-56 — DFIG Piecewise-ODE Model (87L/87G)}
\textbf{Chapter:} Ch.\ 6 \S6.1 \quad \textbf{Target:} IEEE TEC \quad 715 lines

\textbf{Problem:} DFIG fault current has two regimes (RSC-active then
crowbar-fired). SG 4-parameter model produces false-block rate $>30\%$.

\textbf{10-parameter model:}
$\theta = [k_{ibr},\varphi_{pre},t_{cb},I_{fund},\varphi_{fund},I_{nat},\varphi_{nat},\omega_{slip},\tau_s,I_{DC}]$

Physics collinearity reduction: $\tau_{DC} = \tau_s$ (DC and natural-response
share stator transient time constant). Smoothed Heaviside ($\sigma_h = 0.5\,\mathrm{ms}$)
for LM-compatible crowbar transition. CUSUM crowbar detector (2-cycle windows,
2048-point FFT): detection latency $10\,\mathrm{ms}$.

\subsection{TR-57 — Bayesian 3-Hypothesis SPRT (87T IBR)}
\textbf{Chapter:} Ch.\ 4 \quad \textbf{Target:} IEEE TPWRD \quad 934 lines

Per-half-cycle asymmetry index $A_k$ drives a three-hypothesis SPRT
($H_0$: Inrush, $H_1$: IBR fault, $H_2$: CT saturation).
Wald boundaries $\log A = \pm 6.91$ ($\alpha = \beta = 0.001$).
Trip time $20\,\mathrm{ms}$ vs.\ $\geq 60\,\mathrm{ms}$ IEC 60255-111.

\subsection{TR-58 — Generalised EAC + CUEP Bound (Relay 78)}
\textbf{Chapter:} Ch.\ 6 \S6.3 \quad \textbf{Target:} IEEE TPWRD \quad 892 lines

Conservative CUEP lower bound (Proposition~1):
$P_{max}^{eff} = P_{max}^{SG} - \Delta P_{max}^{GFL}$,
$\delta_{LB} = \pi - \arcsin(P_m/P_{max}^{eff}) \leq \delta_u$ (proven).
Conservatism gap $\varepsilon \leq 7.2^\circ$. Standard blinder error:
up to $+6.7^\circ$ (unsafe).
