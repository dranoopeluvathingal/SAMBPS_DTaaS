SECTION 1  IMPEDANCE MODEL PHYSICS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Series-compensated line Π-model:
  Z_line(f) = R + j·(f/f₀)·XL – j·(f₀/f)·XC
  Natural resonance: f_r = f₀·√(XC/XL) = f₀·√(comp%/100)

GFL converter impedance (PI current controller, d-axis):
  Y_conv(s) = (k_p·s + k_i) / (L_f·s² + (k_p+R_f)·s + k_i)
  Re[Z_conv(f)] < 0 when f > f_cross = √(k_i/L_f) / (2π)

SSCI criterion (evaluated at line resonance f_r):
  Re[Z_network(f_r) + Z_conv(f_r)] < –0.005 pu

GFM immunity:
  Z_conv_gfm = R_virt + j·X_virt  (purely positive → no SSCI)
