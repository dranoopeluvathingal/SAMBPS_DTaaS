• MultiSourceEKF uses a 4-state per-source scalar Kalman filter
  (random-walk process model, direct observation) with cross-coupling
  of alpha estimates between voltage-dip-visible sources:
    w_ij = 1 / (1 + d_ij / d_0),  d_0 = 0.10 pu

• MeshedCoordinator IBR reach factor:
    z1_reach = zone1_reach_pu × z_line × (1 – 0.10 × f_gfm × k_ibr)
  GFM current limiting reduces apparent fault current → relay
  under-reaches without compensation; factor accounts for this.

• POTT confirmed as preferred scheme for IBR-dominated meshed
  networks: faster than DCB, more selective than PUTT.
