I am writing to introduce the SAMBPS DTaaS Fault-Location
Identification project and to open an exploratory conversation
about a possible HVDC follow-on track once our Phase-5 AC HIL
validation lands.

### Shared technical context

Our current Phase-5 scope is AC distribution-feeder HIF location
on the IEEE 34-node test feeder (single-ended, training-free,
power-frequency-admittance-based).  HVDC fault location and DC-
side fault classification are NOT in the strict Phase-5 scope —
but Amprion's HVDC operating experience and the recent emphasis
on multi-terminal MMC-HVDC fault discrimination is a natural
extension target for a Phase-6 / Phase-7 follow-on track of the
SAMBPS DTaaS digital-twin-as-a-service programme.

The SAMBPS DTaaS programme as a whole (the wider mother project
sponsoring this thesis at IIT Madras) is building a unified
substation-side digital-twin emission framework that already
covers four distinct AC protection sub-projects (line
differential 87L, transformer differential 87T, bus differential
87B, and HIF location).  An HVDC-side track would be a natural
fifth sub-project — and a partnership with Amprion's HVDC
engineering team would be invaluable for grounding the operating-
condition envelope and the typical test-case suite.

### What we are NOT asking for now

This memo is **explicitly NOT** a request for Amprion HIL access,
data sharing, or any commitment.  The Phase-5 HIL programme is
served by IIT Madras + NUS GEMS + NTU CTSP (the three redundant
AC paths in [`docs/hil_access_matrix.md`](hil_access_matrix.md)).

### What we ARE proposing

A short (30-min) introductory video call at your convenience, to:

1. Introduce the SAMBPS DTaaS programme and its current AC
   sub-project portfolio.
2. Discuss the typical HVDC-side digital-twin emission patterns
   Amprion would find most useful for protection / fault-
   classification R&D collaborations.
3. Sketch a possible Phase-6 / Phase-7 HVDC follow-on track.
4. Identify whether there is a named technical liaison at Amprion
   HVDC who would be the natural point-of-contact for a follow-on
   conversation 12-18 months from now.
