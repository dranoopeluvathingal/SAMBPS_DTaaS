## Reading this diagnostic

A cell appears here when the empirical mean location error, taken over 100 noise realisations, is at least two standard errors above zero.  Causes can include:

* genuine systematic bias of the estimator (most   cases at the boundary or near the CRLB floor);
* model-mismatch bias (the data-generating model is   not the optimiser model);
* near-degenerate cost surface (the optimiser pulls   toward a wrong but locally-optimal cell).

Recommended next steps: cross-check against
`outputs/phase1_crossplatform_results.csv` (per-cell, single-trial) and the WP1.6 corrected CRLB once available.
