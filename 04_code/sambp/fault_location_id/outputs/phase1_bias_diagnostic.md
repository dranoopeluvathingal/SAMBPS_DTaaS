* genuine systematic bias of the estimator (most   cases at the boundary or near the CRLB floor);
* model-mismatch bias (the data-generating model is   not the optimiser model);
* near-degenerate cost surface (the optimiser pulls   toward a wrong but locally-optimal cell).
