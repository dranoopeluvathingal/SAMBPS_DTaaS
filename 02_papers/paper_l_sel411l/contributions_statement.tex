\noindent\textbf{Detailed statement.}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Arun V.\ Eluvathingal (AVE):}
Conceived and implemented the SAMBPS co-processor architecture on the SEL~RTAC~3530.
Derived Proposition~1 (critical-path timing bound) and Proposition~2
(float32 $\kappa_n$ bound) with full perturbation-theory proofs.
Designed and executed all 48 HIL scenarios; characterised deviations D3 and D4.
Implemented IEC~61850 Sampled Value ingestion and GOOSE coordination on the RTAC.
Wrote all sections of the manuscript.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{R.\ K.\ Prasad (RKP):}
Provided research direction and laboratory resources (SEL-411L, SEL~RTAC~3530,
RTDS GPC-III hardware).
Reviewed all technical propositions and validation results.
Edited and approved the final manuscript.
