\section*{Plain-Language Statement (for portal upload)}

\textbf{Anoop V. Eluvathingal:} Conceptualised the field-validation
framework; curated and labelled the 157-recording DSO-A dataset (TR-73);
developed the three field-adaptation corrections F1 (legacy-IBR
$\kappa_n$-trajectory detector), F2 (split-window HIF estimator), and F3
(shape-factor calibration); performed all analyses and Monte Carlo
benchmarks; produced all figures; wrote the manuscript draft.

\textbf{R. K. Prasad:} Supervised the research programme; secured the
research agreement with DSO-A for field-data access; provided guidance on
experimental design and result interpretation; reviewed and edited the
final manuscript.

\bigskip
\noindent\textit{Conflict of interest:} The authors declare no conflict of
interest.  The field dataset was provided under a research agreement with
a distribution network operator; the operator had no role in analysis,
interpretation, or the decision to publish.

\bigskip
\noindent\textit{Data availability:} Anonymised event metadata (timestamps,
fault type, k$_{\rm ibr}$ quartile) will be made available via the IIT
Madras data repository upon acceptance.  Raw DFR waveforms cannot be
shared publicly due to the DSO confidentiality agreement.
