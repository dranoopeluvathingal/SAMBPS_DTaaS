This statement describes the contribution of each author to the
manuscript submitted to \textit{Proceedings of the IEEE}.

\bigskip
\begin{tabular}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{3.8cm}p{10cm}@{}}
\toprule
Author & Contributions \\
\midrule
Arun V.\ Eluvathingal &
  Conceived the five-layer SAMBPS architecture (C16);
  developed the theoretical contributions C1 (adaptive slope),
  C9 (SVD interlacing), and C13 (CUEP bound);
  designed and executed all seven validation campaigns (G1--G7);
  performed all simulations and hardware experiments;
  wrote the complete manuscript. \\[6pt]
S.\ A.\ Soman &
  Supervised the research programme;
  provided critical guidance on protection engineering requirements
  and system-level framing;
  reviewed and revised the manuscript for intellectual content;
  co-approved the final submission. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
