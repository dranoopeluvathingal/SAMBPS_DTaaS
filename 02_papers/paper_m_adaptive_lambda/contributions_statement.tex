\bigskip\noindent\textbf{Detailed statement.}

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Arun V.\ Eluvathingal (AVE):}
Identified the stationarity assumption gap in existing SAMBPS implementations.
Derived the steady-state tracking-bias formula and the tracking--noise
trade-off bound.  Designed the CUSUM detector and logistic $\lambda$-adaptation
law; calibrated parameters over solar diurnal, cloud transient, wind, and
topology-step archetypes.  Proved Proposition~1 (conditioning bound).
Implemented the Monte Carlo framework (5\,000 trials per scenario, 20\,000
total) and analysed all results.  Wrote all sections of the manuscript.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{R.\ K.\ Prasad (RKP):}
Provided research direction and ANDES/RTDS simulation infrastructure.
Reviewed the CUSUM design and stability proof.  Edited and approved the
final manuscript.
